  two 1-ha plots inventoried by the Bridge project.
- [`Paracou618`](https://ericmarcon.github.io/entropart/reference/Paracou618.MC.md)
  : Paracou field station plots 6 and 18, two 1-ha plots inventoried by
  the Bridge project.
- [`Paracou618`](https://ericmarcon.github.io/entropart/reference/Paracou618.Taxonomy.md)
  : Taxonomy (Family - Genus - Species) of Paracou field station plots 6
  and 18, two 1-ha plots inventoried by the Bridge project.
- [`Paracou618`](https://ericmarcon.github.io/entropart/reference/Paracou618.dist.md)
  : Functional distances between pairs of species of Paracou field
  station plots 6 and 18, two 1-ha plots inventoried by the Bridge
  project.
- [`Paracou618`](https://ericmarcon.github.io/entropart/reference/EightSpAbundance.md)
  : Abundances of 8 species to run examples.
- [`Paracou618`](https://ericmarcon.github.io/entropart/reference/EightSpTree.md)
